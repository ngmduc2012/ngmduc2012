# My Wong

My Wong is the notebook where I keep things I explore beyond writing software: a tool, a shortcut, a way to take notes or an approach to learning. Each article is a note I can return to, and something you can try when you have a similar need.

## What I share

* **Tools and utilities:** notes, shortcuts and ways to use tools at work.
* **Learning habits:** organizing learning, taking notes and revisiting what I have studied.
* **AI and automation:** applications of technology that interest me and that I continue to explore.

## How to read these notes

You do not need to read from beginning to end. Choose something you want to do, try the steps in the notes and adapt them to your tools and habits.
