Wong Coupon is where I share what I learn about Buddhist teachings, practice methods and bringing practice into daily life. Alongside the articles, I develop My Peace to help people keep track of their daily chanting.

## Read and learn

The collection introduces basic Buddhist concepts, Pure Land practice and meditation. You can start with the definitions and then choose a subject you would like to explore.

## The My Peace app

**My Peace** is the Android app I develop alongside the Wong Coupon articles. You can start with a manual chanting session, then use voice counting, a timer or progress records as needed.

In the **[introduction to My Peace](https://wong-coupon.gitbook.io/wong-coupon/apps/apps)**, I explain how to get started and what to keep in mind when choosing a counting method.

**[Get My Peace on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=wongcoupon.com.buddha_supper_app&hl=en)**.
