# My Wong

My Wong is my notebook for exploring tools and trying new ways of working. I collect lessons, useful tips and experiences with technology that can make everyday work easier.

**[Explore My Wong](https://wong-coupon.gitbook.io/my-wong)**

## What I share

* **Tools and utilities:** notes, shortcuts and ways to use tools at work.
* **Learning habits:** organizing learning, taking notes and revisiting what I have studied.
* **AI and automation:** applications of technology that interest me and that I continue to explore.

## For curious readers

You can browse My Wong as an open notebook. Pick a subject that interests you, try an approach that fits and adapt it to your own needs.
